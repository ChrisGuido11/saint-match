## The corrected pack

**Topic + theme bridge (replacement)**

> **Virtue:** reviewing the day. Not discernment in the abstract but the specific
> discipline Ignatius wrote down at *Spiritual Exercises* §§32–43 — going back
> over the hours in order, twice a day, about a quarter of an hour, in five
> fixed points (D12). He worked it out on himself while immobilised with a
> shattered leg and nothing to do but notice things (D2, D3, D3a, D4).
>
> **Micro-action (5–15 min):** *Tonight, walk back through today hour by hour
> and name one moment you would do differently. One. Do not fix it, just name
> it.*
>
> **Swap test:** substituting another saint breaks it, because the action is a
> named method from named paragraphs of a book this saint wrote.

**Description caption (replacement)**

```
St Ignatius of Loyola, priest and founder of the Society of Jesus

He was twenty-nine when a cannonball ricocheted off a wall at Pamplona and shattered his right leg. The bone was reset, broken again on purpose and reset a second time, and he limped for the rest of his life. Laid up for months, he asked for the chivalric romances he liked and was brought a life of Christ and a book of saints' lives instead.

What he did with that is the part worth copying. He noticed that the daydreams of glory left him dry once they ended and that the other reading did not, and he started paying attention to the difference. The method he eventually wrote down is unglamorous: twice a day, about a quarter of an hour, go back over the hours in order, in five fixed points.

It is not a technique for hearing God more clearly. It is a technique for finding out what you actually did, which is usually the less welcome question.

St Ignatius of Loyola, priest and founder of the Society of Jesus, pray for us.
```

**Soft CTA (replacement)**

```
One saint, one small challenge, each day. Saint Match — free to start.
```
