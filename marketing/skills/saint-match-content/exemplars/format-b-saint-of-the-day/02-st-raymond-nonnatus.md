# SPECIMEN — St Raymond Nonnatus

**Status:** Saint Match specimen, **drafted in-house, not yet shipped.** It is
the first worked example of Format B in the brand's own register.
**Source:** drafted alongside the Format B spec; illustrative.

Distinguish it from the Format A gold set in `exemplars/`, which is six
**shipped** captions. This one demonstrates the intended shape; it has not been
validated by publication.

---

## Caption

```
Raymond stayed behind so someone else could go free. He never got to explain
why — they padlocked his mouth shut. Some people are carrying something they
can't put words to. St Raymond, pray for us.
```

~38 words. At or just under the floor of the 40–70 band — **W1 would flag this
as borderline**, and it is recorded here honestly rather than padded to fit.
Erring short is the right direction for this format, but a live draft should
land inside the band.

---

## The hinge

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Hinge concept** | **what can't be put into words** |
| **Saint side** | "He never got to explain why — they padlocked his mouth shut." |
| **App side** | "Some people are carrying something they can't put words to." |
| **Stated or performed?** | **Performed.** The app is never named. |

**Saint Match's hinge is the app's actual function:** the saint carried a
specific thing, and the app matches a person to the saint who carried theirs.
Here the carried thing is *the unspeakable* — Raymond's silence made literal by
the padlock, the reader's silence made ordinary. The post does not say "find
your saint". It shows the match happening and stops.

**Swap test:** substitute another saint and it collapses. The padlock is
Raymond's alone; "stayed behind so someone else could go free" is the
Mercedarian fourth vow, not a generic virtue. The hinge is anchored to this
life, which is what W2 requires.

## What it demonstrates

- **Third person.** "Some people", not "you" — the turn toward the reader is
  made without direct address.
- **One fact, one image.** The padlock. No second fact competing with it.
- **No explaining sentence** between the hinge and the invocation. The temptation
  is to add "Saint Match helps you find the saint who understands" — that line
  would flatten the hinge and STOP at W2 and B2-B.
- **Ends on the invocation**, nothing after it.
- **No pitch, no hashtags, no emoji, no scripture block.**

## Sourcing caveat

The dossier discipline of `best_skill.md` §§1–2 applies here exactly as it does
to Format A, and this specimen is **not** a demonstration of it — no dossier is
attached. In a live pack every claim in these four sentences needs a graded
dossier line. The padlock in particular is an **iconographic attribute** and its
grade must be established before it is asserted as flatly as it is here; if it
grades LEGEND, W5 STOPs the post, because at this length the anchoring fact
cannot be a legend even when attributed.

Note also what the post correctly leaves out: **Raymond was never a cardinal**
(`best_skill.md` §8.2). No rank, no red, in the copy or in the image.
