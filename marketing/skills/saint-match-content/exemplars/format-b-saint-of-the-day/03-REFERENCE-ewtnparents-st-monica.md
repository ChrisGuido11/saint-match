## Why it is here: the B-2 shape, isolated

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Carries the teaching** | The **carousel**. Delete the caption and the post is intact. |
| **Hook slide, part 1** | A **numbered promise** — "4 things". A countable contract. |
| **Hook slide, part 2** | An **explicit audience** — "every Catholic parent". |
| **Caption** | Minimal: framing question + invocation, 14 words. |
| **Ending** | The invocation. |

The two hook-slide parts are the whole mechanism, and they are doing different
jobs. The **number** is a contract with a reader who is deciding in half a
second whether to swipe: four is finite, four is countable, four promises the
post ends. The **audience** is a self-selection filter: a reader who is a
Catholic parent knows the post is addressed to them before reading a word of it.
Together they answer "why should I stop?", which is the question `04` and `06`
never answer.

**1,306 saves is the highest figure in this folder** and the gap is widest on
*saves* specifically, not on likes — Clare is within 1% on likes and 33% behind
on saves. A save is a promise to come back, and a countable list of teachings is
a thing you come back to. That is the most useful single observation in the set,
and it is still one post; see the README before leaning on it.

**Opening line, for the record:** the caption opens on the reader's question, and
the hook slide opens on the promise. Neither opens on the calendar. Compare `04`
and `06`.

## Where Saint Match must diverge — the audience line

**"every Catholic parent" is a demographic, and for EWTN Parents that is
correct.** A demographic *is* that account's identity; its whole audience
self-selected on it before the post existed.

**Saint Match matches on struggle, not on demographic.** The app's function is
that a person brings a struggle and is matched to the saint who carried
something like it. So the same slot on a Saint Match hook slide carries a
**struggle topic**:

- "4 things St Monica can teach anyone waiting on someone they love."
- "3 things St Rita can teach anyone carrying a shame they have not said out
  loud."

This is not a criticism of the specimen. It is a statement that **the specimen is
evidence for the slot, not for what fills it.** `SCORER.md` W7 makes the struggle
topic a hard requirement and a demographic a STOP, and the reason is written into
the check: on B-2 the audience line is the **app side of the hinge** (W2). A
demographic there is a segment label, and the post loses its only bridge.

## What is *not* transferable

- **"Please Pray for Us!" with title-case and an exclamation mark.** Saint
  Match's form is "St [Name], pray for us." Punctuation and casing here are
  EWTN's, not a rule (`SCORER.md` W3 tolerates the variation; it does not endorse
  this styling).
- **The per-slide content is not recorded.** Only the hook slide text and the
  caption were captured. **The post runs 7 slides behind a promise of 4, and what
  the other slides do is unknown.** So **no total slide count is prescribed** for
  B-2 (`SCORER.md` §3B.0) — there is nothing here to prescribe from, and
  inventing one would be exactly the kind of unattested structure this folder
  refuses to invent.
- **No sourcing discipline.** As with every third-party specimen: EWTN is under
  no dossier obligation. Under `best_skill.md` §§1–2 each of the four teachings
  would need its own graded dossier line (`SCORER.md` W5, variant B-2 part), and
  Monica's case has a real trap in it — her recorded speech comes from Augustine's
  *Confessions* IX, quotable as TRADITIONAL and attributed "as her son recorded
  it", never as transcript (`best_skill.md` §7.3).
