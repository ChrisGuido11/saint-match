## Why it is here: the hinge, isolated

This is the clearest available demonstration of the mechanic that defines
Format B.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Hinge word** | **broadcast** |
| **Saint side** | The Lord allowed Clare to see Mass "broadcast" on the wall of her room. |
| **App side** | EWTN broadcasts Daily Mass to those who cannot attend. |
| **Stated or performed?** | **Performed.** The link is never explained. |

The whole method is in the repetition of one word. The caption never says "just
like Clare, we bring Mass to you" — it puts the word in quotation marks on the
saint's side, lets the reader notice, and moves on. **The reader completes the
connection, which is why it lands.** An explained hinge is a dead hinge.

Note also what it does *not* do, all of which Format B inherits:

- **~55 words.** Inside the 40–70 band.
- **Third person throughout.** No "you", no exhortation.
- **One fact**, doing all the anchoring work.
- **Ends on the invocation**, and nothing follows it.
- **No pitch.** The brand's presence is the hinge. There is no call to download,
  subscribe or tune in — the connection is the whole CTA.
- **No scripture block, no ✝️, no engagement line, no hashtag wall.** Its one
  hashtag is inline on the brand name, not a block.

## What is *not* transferable

- **The scare quotes on "broadcast"** are doing real work here because the word
  is anachronistic to Clare's century. That is specific to this pairing and
  becomes a tic if repeated.
- **The exclamation mark** on the invocation. Saint Match's specimen uses a full
  stop; either is fine, but do not read EWTN's punctuation as a rule.
- **The fact's grade is unmarked.** EWTN is under no dossier discipline. Under
  `best_skill.md` §2 this vision is at best TRADITIONAL and would need attributing
  phrasing ("the tradition remembers…"), and a Saint Match post resting on it
  flatly asserted would STOP at T2 and W5. **The specimen models the hinge; it
  does not model our sourcing standard.**

Read alongside `03`, `04` and `06`, which are from the same organisation and

which both open "August [N] is the feast day of…".
