Exact entitlements are shown in the App at the time of purchase.

Auto-renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple ID is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends, at the price shown in the App.

How to cancel

  1. Open iPhone Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Saint Match and choose Cancel Subscription.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. Pro stays active until then. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store rules. We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves. To request one, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support.

Billing and data

Apple processes payment. RevenueCat is our entitlement layer: it tells the App whether your Apple ID currently has Pro. We do not receive or store card numbers, billing addresses, or Apple ID passwords. If Pro does not appear after purchase, open Settings in the App and tap Restore Purchases.
