What Saint Match is

You name a struggle (or a joy). The app matches you with a Catholic saint. You get one short practice. That matching is included with the free iPhone download. Optional Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

It is not a game. It is not a parish, a confessional, or official Church teaching.

Getting started

  1. Download on the App Store and open the app.
  2. Walk through the short onboarding.
  3. On Home, pick a mood or tap to write what’s on your heart.
  4. Read the saint. Accept the practice. Do it when you can. Mark it done.
  5. If you want a 9-day prayer, start a novena from a saint you’ve met.

Common questions

Is the download free?

Yes. Saint Match is free to download on iPhone. Matching a struggle to a saint is included. Pro is optional.

What does Pro include?

Unlimited daily saint matches, unlimited active novenas, full Virtue Portfolio analytics, and PDF export. Monthly is $4.99. Yearly is $39.99. Apple bills you and handles cancel and refund.

How do I cancel Pro?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Saint Match. We cannot cancel an Apple subscription for you. If Pro does not show after you paid, use Restore Purchases in the app.

My data vanished after I reinstalled

Anonymous data lives on the device. Link an email in Settings before you switch phones if you want a cloud copy.

A match is slow or failed

Matching needs a network for the AI path. If the server cannot be reached, the app falls back to a local saint library. Force-close and reopen if the screen sticks.

My streak reset

A streak counts consecutive days you finish a practice. Miss more than a day and it starts over, unless a weekly streak freeze applies on a Pro plan. Check the calendar tab for what was actually marked done.

Is this affiliated with the Church?

No. Independent devotion tool. Talk to your priest when you need a pastor, not an app.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete Account. That removes local data and our server copy. It cannot be undone.
