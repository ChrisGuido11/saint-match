2. Description of Service

Saint Match is an iPhone application for personal Catholic devotion. The App lets you name a struggle or mood, match with a Catholic saint, receive one short practice, keep an optional streak and Virtue Portfolio, and pray 9-day novenas. Novenas come in two distinct forms: traditional novenas reproduced word for word from published church sources, and optional AI-composed novenas generated around your own intention. Matching a struggle to a saint and traditional novenas are included with the free download. Some features require a paid Pro subscription.

The App is a private devotion tool. It is not a game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a substitute for the Catholic Church, the sacraments, or pastoral care.
