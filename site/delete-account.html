Account

Delete your account

You can delete your Saint Match account and data from inside the App at any time. If the in-app path fails, email us and we will do it for you.

Delete in the App

  1. Open Saint Match.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

What is removed

  • Local data on the device: matches, practices, streaks, novena progress, reflections, and preferences.
  • Our server copy: the anonymous Supabase account created on first open, any linked email, and any synced rows if you had linked an account.

After deletion we wipe personal data from our servers within 30 days, except where a legal hold requires us to keep a record.

Deletion cannot be undone. There is no recovery window. If you want a copy first, use Settings, then Export My Data.

An Apple subscription is cancelled separately. Deleting your account does not cancel Saint Match Pro. Cancel it in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Saint Match. See the Subscriptions page.

If the in-app path fails

Email support@saintmatch.app or cguido85@icloud.com with the subject line "Delete my account."

  • If you linked an email, write from that address so we can confirm it is you.
  • If you never linked an email, describe the device (for example, iPhone model and roughly when you installed the App) and anything else that helps us find the anonymous account.

We confirm by reply once the deletion is done.
